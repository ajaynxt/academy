Parent & Student FAQs

Answer the questions that delay admission decisions

Can we attend a demo class before admission?

Yes. The demo request should be confirmed by the centre or online academic team based on the relevant course and batch.

How are scholarship benefits decided?

Scholarship eligibility should be based on the published test, result and admission policy. Applicable terms must be provided in writing.

Are online and classroom courses the same?

Course coverage may be similar, but timings, interaction, tests, support and material delivery can differ. Compare the specific programme details.

Are the results and faculty profiles here real?

No. This is a portfolio concept and all result profiles, fees and faculty details must be replaced with verified client information.
